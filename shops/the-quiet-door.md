## The Shop

The front is a respectable and genuinely functioning glover's, and most people who come here buy gloves. Past the fitting curtain is a second room with no window, lit by one shuttered lantern, where the goods are laid on green baize like a jeweler's and nothing carries a tag. The room is quiet in a built way — double walls, sawdust in the cavity — and you notice the street noise stop as the curtain falls.

## The Merchant

Sennet Vey has been introduced to you by someone, because there is no other way to arrive. Small, immaculate, with a glover's fingers and an auctioneer's memory, and he addresses everyone by the name they gave, exactly, every time. He wants to know who is buying acid in the engravers' quarter, and he will trade for that. *"I don't sell to strangers and I don't sell in a hurry. If you're in a hurry, that's an argument for coming back tomorrow."*

## Behind the Counter

- Prices are high and barely move, but he will take information in part-payment and value it generously and out loud, which is itself a kind of test.
- He asks nothing about intent and everything about timing: when, where, and who else knows you're buying. Refuse to answer and the curtain opens again.
- A customer who names their introducer wrongly is served gloves, politely, at full price, and never gets past the curtain again.

## Hooks

Someone has commissioned Sennet, through three separate intermediaries, to source a mask of plain faces — and specified that it must be a *used* one, with the hollows already worn into the lining by a previous wearer. He has been offered four times the going rate. He has not taken the commission, and he has started asking his own suppliers who else has been asking.
