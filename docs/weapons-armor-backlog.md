# Weapons & Armor Backlog

The second content wave: **50 items** (30 weapons, 20 armor & shields) for `items/weapons/` and `items/armor/`. Written per the same three spec docs as the v0.1 backlog: `docs/item-template.md`, `docs/style-guide.md`, `docs/pricing-guide.md`, plus the two rules below.

**Status key:** ✅ merged · 🔨 claimed/in progress · ⬜ open

## The Two Rules of This Wave

1. **Regional variants never change combat stats.** A coastal-forged longsword is *statistically identical* to the SRD longsword: same damage, same properties, same price at common quality. What varies is everything else — materials, silhouette, maintenance needs, cultural weight, what its presence says about its bearer, and situational non-combat texture (a hooked blade that aids climbing checks; a blade that resists salt corrosion). The variant is a *biography*, not a buff. Front matter uses `subcategory:` for the base SRD weapon (e.g., `subcategory: longsword`).
2. **Quality tiers follow the existing contract, applied to arms.** Shoddy: risk of breakage/malfunction at a discount. Fine: durability, maintenance, or handling texture — never attack, damage, or AC. Masterwork: fine made reliable, plus a mark and held resale. Only *magic* items touch combat math, and none appear in this wave.

## Brief Addendum for This Wave

Everything in the v0.1 backlog's writer brief applies, plus:

1. **Stats stay SRD.** In the `mechanics` fields, state the base weapon/armor and its unchanged core stats in each system's vocabulary, then any *non-combat* situational texture. When in doubt, write less mechanics, more biography.
2. **subcategory = base SRD item**, exactly as spelled in this backlog.
3. **Regional flavor stays neutral.** Coastal, highland, steppe, marsh — geography types, never named places, per the style guide.
4. **Arms carry politics.** The best hooks in this wave come from what carrying the thing *means*: armory stamps, dueling law, guild colors, clergy issue, poaching statutes. Use it.
5. **Batch variety check:** across a batch, vary who made it, who's allowed to carry it, and what it costs socially — not just what it is.

Update this file's status column in the same PR that adds the items.
