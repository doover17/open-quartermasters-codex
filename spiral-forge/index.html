Final map → VTT

Attach your finished Inkarnate map. The Forge adds walls, doors, light sources and sight-blocking pillars from this layout and packages it all as one Universal VTT file. Roll20 imports it directly, Foundry with the Universal Battlemap Importer module, and Owlbear Rodeo with the Smoke & Spectre extension.

Saves a .zip with a .dd2vtt inside: unzip it, then import the .dd2vtt. Secret doors come through as closed walls, so players can't see them; switch them to secret doors in your VTT. Multi-level places come out as one .dd2vtt per level: with All levels shown you get every level in the zip; with an attached map, pick its level first. With no map attached, the draft map is used, so you can test the walls now.
