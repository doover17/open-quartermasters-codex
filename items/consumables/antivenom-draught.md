## Description

A murky brown draught of charcoal, milk thistle, and rendered serpent gland, gritty on the tongue and cold all the way down. It quiets the burn in a bitten limb within minutes — quiets, not ends. The wax seal on every honest bottle is stamped with the fang of whatever venom went into it.

## At the Table

- The draught turns "she dies on the trail" into "she dies unless you reach a healer by dawn." That's a chase, not a funeral.
- Reading the fang-stamps on an apothecary's shelf tells the party exactly what hunts the surrounding country.

## Hooks

Someone has to milk the snakes. The one-eyed woman who supplies every apothecary in the valley takes on a new apprentice each spring, and no one can name a former one.
