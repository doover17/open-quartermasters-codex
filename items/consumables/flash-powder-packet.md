## Description

A twist of stiff paper the weight of a coin, gritty inside like fine sand, smelling of struck matches before it has ever been struck. It goes off not with a bang but with a light so total the world becomes a held breath. Stagehands invented the mix for summoning scenes; the twist-pattern of the paper still tells you which playhouse the recipe escaped from.

## At the Table

- The entrance: the packet hits the flagstones, and when the audience can see again, someone is standing in the room who wasn't.
- The exit is the same trick run backward — and the party shouting "eyes!" to each other first is the round everyone remembers.

## Hooks

Close your eyes during the flash and you keep your sight; that much everyone knows. A festival conjurer in the harbor quarter has stopped performing because, she swears, the last time she looked away, the afterimage on her eyelids was of the room with one extra person in it.
