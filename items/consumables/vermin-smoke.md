## Description

A gray cone the size of a spinning top, smelling of bitter herbs and something sharper underneath that makes your eyes prickle before it's even lit. It burns from the tip down in a slow ashen spiral, and everything small and many-legged decides at once to be elsewhere. Granary wardens stamp the base of theirs with the harvest year — a cellar cleared with old stock is a cellar not cleared at all.

## At the Table

- The cellar's carpet of rats parts like a curtain, which is when everyone sees what the rats were covering.
- Lighting one before camping in a ruin is the veteran move that tells new players who at this table has been bitten before.

## Hooks

The last granary warden to burn a cone in the undercroft came up the stairs at a run and paid a mason, that same afternoon, to brick the doorway shut.
