## Description

Shave a corner into boiling water and the smell arrives first — scorched earth, dark chocolate, and something faintly of leather — followed by a taste that is all edge: bitter, gritty, and unapologetic. The bricks travel from somewhere south of every map your party owns, stamped with a shipping mark in a script none of you read. Habitués learn to gauge a caravan's route by the flavor of its bricks.

## At the Table

- The night-watch communion: two characters who would never talk by daylight, hunched over one steaming cup at the third hour.
- A character out of coffee three days from anywhere is a mood the whole table will feel.

## Hooks

Ask the dockside grocer why this month's bricks are half price and he changes the subject. The shipping mark on the new stock is the old mark drawn mirror-backward, and the caravan that delivered them arrived with one more wagon than it left with.
