## Description

The leaf is cut coarse and packs down under the thumb with a give that tells an old smoker whether it's been kept damp enough; too dry and it burns hot and fast, too wet and it sulks. The ritual matters more than the leaf — the packing, the passing, the silence between draws. Growers mark their harvest with a colored thread through the pouch string, and arguments over which valley's thread is best have outlived the arguers.

## At the Table

- The universal stage business for a slow scene: two characters, one pipe, and the questions that only come out sideways.
- Offering the first draw to a stranger is a truce older than most treaties; refusing it politely is a skill of its own.

## Hooks

An old campaigner in the market square trades a bowl of his private blend for stories — one bowl, one story, no coin accepted. He never smokes his own bowl until the teller is out of sight, and he writes nothing down, and he has been asking for stories about one particular road.
