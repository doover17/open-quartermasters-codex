## Description

A dried gourd of amber sap that smells of pine and spoiled honey, heavy and slow when you tilt it, like a thought it hasn't finished. It pours in one reluctant sheet and grips anything that presses into it — boot, hoof, or hand. Poachers score the gourds with a thumbnail cross so they can tell theirs from a rival's at a glance.

## At the Table

- Poured across the doorway behind a fleeing party, it converts one round of running into a whole scene of swearing.
- Laid before a fight, it turns the obvious charge lane into a trap the players designed themselves.

## Hooks

Every gourd sold this season grips harder than last year's stock, and the herbalist who taps the trees will not say what she has been feeding them.
