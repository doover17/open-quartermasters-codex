## Description

A rope of cured leaf as long as your forearm, twisted tight, sticky with molasses and smelling of sweet smoke and cellar dark. Out past the last mile-marker it spends better than silver — coin needs a market, but a pipe needs only an evening. Sutlers score each twist into thumb-widths, because that is the denomination in which favors are made.

## At the Table

- The gate guard's price isn't posted, but everyone on the river knows it: two thumb-widths, cut with your own knife, offered before you ask anything.
- Sharing a pipe afterward slows a scene down on purpose — information arrives at the speed of smoke.

## Hooks

The ferry guards, the toll-keeper, and the hermit at the pass all take twist tobacco — and all of them, without conferring, refuse the batch tied with blue thread. None of them can say who warned them off it.
