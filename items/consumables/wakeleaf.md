## Description

Narrow silver-green leaves that crackle like paper and taste of pepper and green walnut husk. Your heart notices before your head does. Career sentries chew it until their gums stain gray at the line — a mark garrison sergeants check for at muster, the way they check boots.

## At the Table

- The all-night vigil, the third watch, the ambush the party refuses to sleep through: wakeleaf makes staying awake a choice with a bill attached.
- A gray-gummed stranger who never seems to sleep raises a fair question: what are they afraid of missing?

## Hooks

Why does the night warden at the granary go through a pouch a night, every night, when nothing has been stolen in years? Her requisition slips stopped saying what she's watching for.
