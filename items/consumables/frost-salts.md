## Description

Coarse blue-white crystals in an oilskin packet, hissing faintly when the seal breaks, cold enough to sting through a glove. Scattered on flame they don't smother it so much as cancel it, leaving a smell like a river in midwinter. Ship chandlers rate a vessel's galley by how many packets hang beside the stove.

## At the Table

- The burning warehouse stops being a countdown when someone remembers what's in the satchel.
- A frosted lock shatters instead of clicking — quieter than a crowbar, colder than picking, and it leaves evidence any locksmith will recognize.

## Hooks

Who froze the vault door from the inside? The salts leave their river-water smell for a day, and the only person who was in the vault that morning has been dead for three.
