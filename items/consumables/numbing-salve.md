## Description

A gray-green paste of clove oil, mandrake leaf, and beeswax that tingles cold on your fingertips even through the wooden spatula. Where it touches, sensation folds up and leaves. Barber-surgeons scrape the jar with a marked stick — every workshop's stick shows the same worn groove at the two-dose line, the amount one soldier needs.

## At the Table

- The arrow has to come out on the kitchen table, tonight. This jar decides whether that scene is screaming or conversation.
- A numbed hand can grip a brand, a blade, or a rope of burning pitch without flinching — and won't notice the damage it's taking.

## Hooks

An interrogator in the low quarter buys the salve in bulk, and not for kindness: her subjects feel nothing while she works, and she describes each cut aloud. The ones she releases never speak of it above a whisper.
