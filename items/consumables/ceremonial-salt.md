## Description

It smells of the sea it came from and of the juniper smoke it was dried over, and on the tongue it blooms — brine first, then a mineral sweetness that plain table salt never carries. The crystals are coarse, gray-white, and cut with flecks of dried herb according to rites older than the towns that practice them. Old cellars for holding it pass down through families, dented and polished by generations of thresholds.

## At the Table

- Bread and salt at the door: the scene where enemies must sit at one table, and everyone watches the cellar instead of each other.
- Sworn over salt, a promise gains witnesses no one can see — and players treat it accordingly.
- Spilling it during the rite stops the room cold.

## Hooks

Three households along the border road have stopped offering salt to travelers this season. Not one of them will say why, and the salter who supplies all three has taken to tasting each shipment himself before it leaves his shop, and spitting it out.
