## Description

A finger-length glass vial of amber liquid that reeks of vinegar and crushed juniper the moment you thumb the stopper. One dose turns a gallon of green marsh water briefly cloudy, then clear, with a gray sediment settling like ash. Mission-posts hand vials out along fever rivers, each one etched with a tally of the outbreaks it has outlived.

## At the Table

- The swamp stops being a wall and becomes a road: the party can drink where pursuers can't follow.
- Ten doses, six people, a two-week crossing — the arithmetic on the vial is a quiet countdown the whole table can feel.

## Hooks

A downriver village now orders the drops by the crate — every household, every week, no explanation. The alchemist who fills the order has stopped drinking from his own well.
