## Description

A wax-paper ball no bigger than a plum, packed so tight it creaks between your fingers, smelling faintly of pitch and burnt sugar. Crush it and the smoke arrives all at once, gray-white and greasy, clinging to wool for days. Stage companies buy them in bulk, which is why half the pellets in circulation are dyed a theatrical crimson.

## At the Table

- The exit line: one round of chaos is exactly enough time to drop through the trapdoor, and everyone at the table knows it.
- Thrown at an archer's feet instead of your own, it turns a killing lane into a guessing game.

## Hooks

Ask any fence who makes the good ones and you get the same answer: a widow who sells only at night, only in singles, and only to people she has watched cross the square twice.
