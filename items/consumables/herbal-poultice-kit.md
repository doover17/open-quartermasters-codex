## Description

A waxed linen roll holding five cloth squares packed with comfrey, plantain leaf, and honey-soaked moss, warm-smelling even cold. Bound over a wound, the poultice draws heat through the night and comes away brown and stiff by morning. Most kits are sewn by the same hands that will one day lay their buyers out, and the stitching is done with the same care.

## At the Table

- Camp stops being downtime when someone has to boil water, change dressings, and sit with the wounded. The kit hands the quiet character a role.
- Five poultices, six wounded. Somebody makes the call.

## Hooks

Every kit the midwife sells includes a sixth square, folded separately, unlabeled, and smelling of nothing at all. She says it's for the wound you don't show anyone.
