## Description

A few amber drops under the tongue, tasting of valerian root, honey, and something faintly like church dust. Warmth settles over your chest like a blanket pulled up by someone else. Widows' cupboards across the hill country hold a bottle worn smooth at the stopper, refilled for a generation and never discussed.

## At the Table

- Before the vault door, the seance, the thing in the well: who drinks, and who wants to feel it coming?
- Dosing a panicking hostage, witness, or horse is legitimate first aid. Dosing a reluctant bride is something else, and the bottle looks the same.

## Hooks

Three of the night watch drink it before every patrol along the old wall, and their reports have become strangely serene — including the one describing, without alarm, the tall figure that walks beside them from the third bell to the fourth.
