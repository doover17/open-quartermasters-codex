## Description

A waxed canvas roll that smells of beeswax and saddle soap, holding two needles, a hank of tarred thread, an awl, and five patches graded from soft glove-leather to sailcloth. The thread drags through your fingers with a workmanlike stickiness and holds a knot like a grudge. Old kits carry the mends of their mends — a patch on the roll itself is the mark of gear that has earned its keep.

## At the Table

- The waterskin gashed at noon is mended by the ten-minute rest, and nobody dies of the desert three days later — expedition drama measured in thread.
- Patching a companion's cloak by firelight, unasked, is a whole friendship in one bullet of downtime.

## Hooks

One tinker's kits each include a sixth patch of soft gray leather, finer than the rest, free of charge. He can't recall where he buys it. Neither can his supplier.
