## Description

A paper packet of elderflower, yarrow, and shaved gingerroot that fills the sickroom with a sharp, honeyed steam. It sweats the fever out through the blankets; the smell clings to the wool for weeks. Country families keep the used packets folded in the family book, one for each illness survived.

## At the Table

- Sitting up all night spooning tea into a dying friend is a scene. A single Medicine roll is not.
- When plague rumors start, the price of these packets is the first thing in town to move.

## Hooks

The hedge-witch swears her recipe hasn't changed in thirty years, but this season's packets smell wrong — sweeter, almost floral — and the people drinking them recover faster than they should.
