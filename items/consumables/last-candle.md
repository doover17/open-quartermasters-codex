## Description

A slim candle of graded wax, ringed with a single black line at the shoulder and smelling faintly of myrrh. Chandlers burn one candle from every batch against a sandglass and stamp the base of its siblings only if it dies on the hour — a common candle costs a copper, but a candle you can swear by costs more. Watch-houses, vigils, and card games all keep time by the same small blue farewell.

## At the Table

- "We leave when the candle dies" turns an hour of dungeon time into a physical object on the table — and the blue minute is the tensest sixty seconds in the game.
- Lighting one over a bargain, a confession, or a goodbye gives any scene a deadline the players can watch burn.

## Hooks

The gaol's night-warden times confessions by last-candle: you have until it dies to talk, and his candles are known to run merciful and long. He buys them from a chandler whose shop no one has ever found open.
