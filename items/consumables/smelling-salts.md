## Description

A stubby glass vial of ammoniac crystals and crushed hartshorn under a cork you learn to loosen one-handed. Unstoppered, it hits like a slap made of smell — eyes water at arm's length. Duelists' seconds carry one on a neck cord, and an old vial's cork bears a tooth-mark for every morning it was needed.

## At the Table

- The wizard is face-down, the ritual is at the third verse, and one round is the whole negotiation. Pop the cork.
- Passing the vial under a "sleeping" sentry's nose settles the question of whether the drink was drugged — publicly, and in one breath.

## Hooks

A collier's boy pulled from the flooded shaft coughed, breathed, and slept — and has slept for nine days since. His mother has bought salts from every shop in town, and last market-day she started buying them in other towns.
