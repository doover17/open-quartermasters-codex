## Description

A dense brick of sawdust, tallow, and pine resin, pressed hard enough to ring when you rap it and greasy enough to leave a shine on your palm. It smells of turpentine and candle stubs — chandlers press them from the sweepings of everything else they make. The scorched thumbprint stamped into one end is the presser's guarantee that it will light.

## At the Table

- Third day of rain, everyone soaked, exhaustion looming: the character who packed a fire-log is briefly the most important person in the wilderness.
- A guaranteed fire is also a guaranteed signal — one brick can announce the party to everything downwind of its resin smoke.

## Hooks

The chandler who presses the best logs buys her resin from one supplier, after dark, and pays whatever he asks without counting. Lately her logs burn with a faint smell of incense — the kind burned only at funerals.
