## Description

The box smells of caramel and rosewater, and each figure dissolves on the tongue into a sweetness with one hidden note — anise in the dancer, ginger in the knight, something like rain in the swan. They are spun sugar, thin as eggshell, tinted with berry juice and poured into molds carved by confectioners who guard the patterns like heirlooms. Held to the light, a good figure glows.

## At the Table

- The cheapest miracle in the pack: produced at the right moment, it stops a child's tears, a mob's youngest thrower, or a fey court's laughter — briefly.
- Twelve figures, twelve small debts a clever player can spend one at a time.

## Hooks

What does the confectioner's newest mold depict? Buyers describe it differently — a tower, a gate, a figure waving — but every child who eats one has the same dream that night, and lately a few of them wave back.
