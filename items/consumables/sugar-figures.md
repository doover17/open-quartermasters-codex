## Description

Each figure weighs nothing in the palm and chimes faintly against its neighbors when the box is tilted — spun sugar, thin as eggshell, tinted with berry juice and poured into molds carved by confectioners who guard the patterns like heirlooms. On the tongue each dissolves into a sweetness with one hidden note: anise in the dancer, ginger in the knight, something like rain in the swan. Held to the light, a good figure glows.

## At the Table

- The cheapest miracle in the pack: produced at the right moment, it stops a child's tears, a mob's youngest thrower, or a fey court's laughter — briefly.
- Twelve figures, twelve bought moments — and a clever player spends them one at a time, knowing exactly how long a sweet lasts.

## Hooks

What does the confectioner's newest mold depict? Buyers describe it differently — a tower, a gate, a figure waving — but every child who eats one has the same dream that night, and lately a few of them wave back.
