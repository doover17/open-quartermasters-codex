## Description

The pouch opens with a punch of smoke, black pepper, and rendered fat that carries across a room — and farther than that on a night wind. Each strip chews slow and salty with a heat that builds at the back of the throat, cut from whatever the smokehouse hung that season. Hunters spice it hard on purpose: a scent that loud reads as food, not intruder, to every nose downwind.

## At the Table

- The classic beat, earned honestly: the estate's hounds come baying around the corner, and one player is already reaching for the pouch.
- Eating your own escape plan on day four of a lean crossing is a decision players remember making.

## Hooks

The smokehouse keeper pays double for game brought in from the western ridge and asks each hunter the same question: did anything follow you down? He smokes that meat in a separate shed, sells it to no one, and burns the drippings.
