## Description

A palm-sized coil of drawn brass wire, bright where your fingers have worked it and dull where it has waited in the pouch. It kinks if you rush it and sings a thin note when a loop pulls true. Village trappers braid a hair from their first catch into the binding cord — wire that has fed someone once, they say, remembers how.

## At the Table

- Rations run out three days before the map does; the snare coil turns that from a crisis into a routine, and the party's camp into a place with work to do at dusk.
- Checking the snare line at dawn is a scene generator: sometimes breakfast, sometimes tracks, sometimes something has checked the line first.

## Hooks

The trapper sells the coil cheap and asks one thing in return: set nothing north of the split oak. His own snares up there come back sprung, licked clean, and neatly re-set.
