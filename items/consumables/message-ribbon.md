## Description

Five pale strips of tight-woven silk, boiled in oil and size until they take charcoal like slate and shed water like a duck. Unrolled, one crackles softly and smells of lamp oil and old rain. Couriers on the long routes trust them over paper the way sailors trust tar over paint — every ribbon carries a scrivener's counter-stamp at one end, so a forged drop is a hanging matter somewhere.

## At the Table

- Fifty words, waterproof, tied where only your ally will look: the party can now run schemes across distance and weather, and so can everyone else.
- A found ribbon is evidence with a texture — who ties a message into a dead man's bootlace?

## Hooks

The drop-tree above the ford holds nine ribbons, all addressed to the same name, none collected. The newest knot is fresh this morning — and the ford has been washed out for a month.
