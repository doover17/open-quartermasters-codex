## Description

A finger-length glass vial, cool to the touch even after hours in a pocket, with two liquids lying in lazy layers until shaken into a moon-pale glow. It gives off no heat, no smoke, and no smell — which is exactly what a miner wants three levels down where the air itself can catch. Spent vials are pierced and worn as pendants in pit towns, one per season survived.

## At the Table

- The light you can use while soaked, while hiding a heat source, or while standing in air that would turn a torch into a funeral.
- Six hours of dim light is a whole watch: hang it over the camp and let the party argue about who sleeps under the glow.

## Hooks

A deep-shaft foreman has started buying spent vials back at double what a full one costs. He wants them unopened, unwashed, and he checks each one against his tongue.
