## Description

Tap it and the yard fills with malt, orchard fruit, and yeast still faintly alive; the first pull tastes of toasted bread and honey with a green-apple snap that gets a crowd talking louder within the hour. Brewers hold back their best autumn barrels for exactly this — the cask a stranger buys for a town — and chalk the buyer's name on the head, where it stays until the rain takes it. Some yards keep the empty cask for years.

## At the Table

- The party rolls into a wary village, and one player says the five best words in the genre: "Drinks are on us tonight."
- By the second hour of a cask night, everyone's secrets sit closer to the surface — including the party's.
- Standing a cask for the workers a villain has been squeezing is a declaration of war that never draws a blade.

## Hooks

The brewer will sell the party any cask but one — the barrel at the back with no chalk name, brewed the autumn the old mayor vanished, tapped by nobody in nine years. He rolls it a quarter turn every month, and last month it was warm.
