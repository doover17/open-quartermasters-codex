## Description

A squat clay vial of mustard water, powdered ipecac root, and brine, smelling exactly like what it's about to make you do. It works fast, loud, and without dignity. Taster's guilds issue one to every member on the first day, along with the advice stitched inside the kit: better emptied than buried.

## At the Table

- The poisoned banquet is a race: who realizes in time, who has a vial, and who's too proud to use it in front of the duke's guests.
- The smuggler who swallowed the evidence knows the party knows. Producing the vial ends the standoff without a blade drawn.

## Hooks

A pickpocket in the fish market swallowed a signet ring two days ago and has since drunk four purgatives. All four came up empty, and she can feel the ring moving — upward.
