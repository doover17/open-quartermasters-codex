## Description

Three sticks of chalk the color of old bone, cool to the touch and faintly gritty with something that sparks pale green under your nail. A fresh mark hangs in the dark like a scratch on the night itself, then dies down to plain white dust — quarry crews call the fading hour "the lease." It writes with the same squeak as its ordinary cousin and vanishes under a wet thumb the same way.

## At the Table

- The rearguard can read the last hour's marks without striking a light — a retreat through absolute dark, navigated at a walking pace by dying green arrows.
- The glow cuts both ways: for one hour, every mark the party makes is a beacon to whatever else is down there reading.

## Hooks

Quarrymen swear the marks keep their glow longer the deeper the gallery. On the lowest level, where the pumps gave out years ago, marks cut the spring before last are still burning.
