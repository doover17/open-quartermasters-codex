## Description

An unremarkable blade with a walnut grip, worn pale where a thumb rides — until it's drawn, when the air around it fills with the warm brown smell of cinnamon. The scent is strongest in the first moment of the draw and fades as the blade stays out, so a fight smells of it once and then not at all. Near the recently dead it doesn't fade.

## At the Table

- The quirk is a free plot sensor: the party's dagger "smells stronger" in a room where the floor was mopped this morning.
- Collectors pay a premium for named, quirked weapons over anonymous +1s — which makes selling it a negotiation rather than a transaction.

## Hooks

Spicebiter has changed hands eleven times, and its previous owners share one detail: not one of them sold it. Each swears it was stolen, or that they woke and it was gone from a locked room. The dealer who has it now sleeps with it under the counter, and has begun leaving the counter unlocked.
