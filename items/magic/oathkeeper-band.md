## Description

Two plain bands of soft red gold, sized to no particular finger and comfortable on any. Worn, a band draws in a fraction — a small deliberate tightening you feel below the knuckle — whenever the other wearer says your name aloud, wherever they are. Long-paired wearers stop noticing it the way you stop noticing a heartbeat, and notice immediately when a day goes by without one.

## At the Table

- Knowledge without enforcement is the cruelest possible design, and players understand it instantly: you will know, you will not be able to prove it, and you will not be able to stop it.
- Give the second band to an NPC and the party has a live wire running out of the campaign into someone else's life.

## Hooks

Bands are made in pairs and buried in pairs, and a single band on the market means one of two things. This one came in from an estate with its twin listed on the same inventory line, struck through in a different ink. The seller can produce the inventory, the strike-through, and a receipt. He cannot produce the twin, and the band he is selling has been tightening, on and off, all week.
