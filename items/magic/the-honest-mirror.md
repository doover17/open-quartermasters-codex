## Description

A standing glass in a plain black frame, taller than a person, and it is exactly blood-warm to the touch at every hour and in every weather — a wall of glass that feels like a held hand. What it gives back is not flattering and not cruel. It is only accurate, which people find they were not prepared for, and the room it stands in is always quieter than the room next door.

## At the Table

- One object that ends every disguise in the campaign is a scalpel, not a hammer: the party has to get the suspect in front of it, which is an entire heist.
- It shows and does not dispel. Knowing the ambassador's true shape while everyone else sees the ambassador is the worst possible position, and the best possible session.

## Hooks

The mirror has been owned by four courts and each of them stopped using it within a year, for reasons the inventories record as *disuse*. It is currently crated, face-in, in a collector's store-room, and the collector has never had it uncrated. He bought it after seeing it once, at a reception, at a distance, across a crowded floor — and what he says he saw was the room reflected correctly with one person too many in it.
