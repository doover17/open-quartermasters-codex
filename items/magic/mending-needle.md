## Description

A slim steel needle blued almost to black, with an eye so small that no thread anyone owns will pass through it. Set it down on torn cloth at night and by morning the tear is gone, closed in stitching finer than the garment ever deserved. Sit up and watch it, though, and it lies there being a needle — it has never once moved while anyone was looking, and every owner has tried.

## At the Table

- The party's kit stops degrading, and the fighter's cloak slowly becomes a history of perfect seams over ruined wool.
- Somebody will stay up to watch it. That scene is the whole reason the item exists, and it should always end with them falling asleep.

## Hooks

Tailors' guilds forbid apprentices to own one, on the grounds that a needle which sews unwatched teaches nothing. This one came out of a master tailor's estate, her tools sold off in a separate lot to a separate buyer. The inventory doesn't list the needle among the tools. It lists it with her jewelry.
