## Description

A stub of a shaker in tarnished pewter, gritty around the perforations, with a screw base nobody has ever managed to turn. What comes out is coarse gray salt that tastes of salt first and then, a beat later, of cold open water — a second note that stays in preserved food for as long as the food lasts, which is forever. Every cook who owns one learns to season around it.

## At the Table

- The ration clock stops. A wilderness arc built on a countdown needs a new source of pressure, and the party has bought its way out of the oldest one in the genre.
- A shaker like this makes the party quartermasters to whoever they meet next: a besieged garrison, a famine village, a becalmed ship. What they charge for it is a character sheet.

## Hooks

A sealed larder came to light this spring under a collapsed manor house, and everything inside was edible — bread, a dressed fowl, a bowl of cut fruit, none of it a day old by taste. The manor came down forty years ago with the family still in it. The salt cellar on the shelf was empty, and the larder door had been barred from the inside.
