## Description

Blackthorn worn to a shine, shod in iron at the foot and cut for someone a hand taller than most. Stand it against any wall, in any building, on any ground, and it leans — never falling, never straightening, always tipping a few degrees toward the same quarter of the horizon. It isn't downhill, it isn't north, and it isn't toward water. It has been the same direction for as long as anyone has kept track.

## At the Table

- Free compass rose, no mechanics required: the party can note the lean in every room they sleep in and start drawing lines on a map themselves.
- The staff propped in the corner of an inn, leaning, is a background detail players will bring up eleven sessions later.

## Hooks

Three pilgrim houses keep records of staves like this and the entries are always the same shape: a name, a direction, and a year the walking stopped. This staff's owner is recorded as having walked its lean for six years and then turned around. She got home, lived a long time, and left instructions that the staff be sold to a stranger rather than kept in the family.
