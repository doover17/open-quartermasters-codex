## Description

A plain canvas dispatch bag with a bone toggle, no deeper inside than out, and it takes whatever you put in it. What it will not do is keep things where you left them: reach in for the rope and find it under the spare boots, which are under a lantern that was on top an hour ago. Everything is always there. Nothing is ever where you last had it.

## At the Table

- Carrying capacity stops being the constraint, which frees a party to haul the statue, the evidence, and the fourth share — and gives the DM permission to hand them something enormous.
- The rummage is the joke and the tension both: the round spent finding the right vial is a round the satchel chose to cost them.

## Hooks

Sold as a courier's bag, and couriers won't have them — a satchel that reorders itself can't be sworn to in front of a magistrate. This one came from a chandlery clearance with a length of chain still buckled through the toggle and a customs seal on the chain, unbroken, from a port that stopped issuing that seal a long time ago.
