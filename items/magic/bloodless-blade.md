## Description

A short leaf-shaped blade, grey and matte, that takes no shine however it is worked. Wounds it makes do not bleed — the edges part, stay parted, and show clean and dry down to whatever is underneath. They also do not close: a cut from this blade knits no faster than the flesh around it decides to, and healed ones stay visible as pale open-looking seams for the rest of a life.

## At the Table

- A weapon that leaves permanent, recognizable marks means the party's history walks around on other people. Someone will come back with a scar and a good memory.
- It makes no one bleed out, which is quietly humane and quietly horrible: the blade is the interrogator's weapon of choice for exactly that reason, and everyone knows it.

## Hooks

There are perhaps forty people alive with the seams, and they find each other — there is a word for it in two languages and a house in the capital where they drink for free. None of them agree on how many blades there are. What they agree on is that the marks are all identical, all the same width, and that two of them were made in the same room on the same night three hundred miles apart.
