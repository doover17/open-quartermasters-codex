## Description

A long key in white metal, plain in the bow and elaborate in the bit, and it is a little longer every time you look — not enough to measure against itself, but measure it against a doorframe you know and the difference is there. The reshaping happens overnight and leaves the bit warm and slightly out of true, so the first turn of a morning always catches once before it gives.

## At the Table

- The key is a plot generator that runs on its own schedule. Wherever it points next is a place the party has been, which means the DM gets to reopen a location the players thought they were done with.
- The best sessions are the ones where it chooses badly: the key that now opens a friend's front door, or a cell the party once got out of.

## Hooks

Keys like this are the reason certain families change their locks on a fixed schedule for reasons they no longer remember. This one's previous owner kept a list of every lock it ever chose — forty-one of them, in eleven years, in his own careful hand. The last entry is not a lock. It is a date, three weeks in the future from when he wrote it, and he wrote it in the same column as the others.
