## Description

Low, soft-soled boots in oxblood leather with a hooked toe, comfortable from the first mile. Behind you the ground closes over itself — snow smooths, mud fills, dust settles — and you can turn and watch it happen at the edge of the light. Your prints do get made. They surface somewhere else: a mile off, a day later, a clear line of your tread crossing a field you have never walked.

## At the Table

- Pursuit stops working, which means the DM has to chase the party with something other than a tracker — and the party feels clever for a whole session.
- The displaced prints are the price. Somewhere, someone is finding the party's footprints in places that make no sense, and drawing conclusions.

## Hooks

A hunting lodge has been logging a trespasser for two years: the same tread, always crossing the same three fields, never entering or leaving them, no prints at the boundaries. They have staked out the fields eleven times. Last spring the line of prints crossed a field on the far side of a river the tread never touched.
