## Description

A ship's lantern in pitted bronze, its glass clouded to the color of a winter sky, heavier than bronze accounts for. The flame inside makes a sound — held close, it is the small continuous hiss of rain falling on a river, a long way off, and it does not change whether the lantern is lit in a dry cellar or forty feet down.

## At the Table

- Every body of water on the map becomes a place with a history the party can read. Harbors, wells, flooded galleries, and the ford where the caravan was lost all have something on the bottom.
- What it shows is where things *came to rest*, not where they went in. The gap between those two facts is most of a mystery.

## Hooks

Salvage crews on this coast bid against each other for a week's hire of a drowned lantern, and one crew has bid every year for nine years and never worked a wreck. They take it to the same stretch of open water, hold it down on a line for one night, and bring it up. They pay in advance, they return it clean, and the youngest of them has started asking the older ones what they are counting.
