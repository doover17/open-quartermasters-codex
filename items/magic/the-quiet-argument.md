## Description

A slim court blade with a swept guard and a grip bound in black silk, and it makes no sound. Not a muffled sound — none: it leaves the scabbard silently, it takes a parry silently, and steel that strikes it rings alone, one voice where there should be two. Fencing halls find this harder to be around than they expect, and most masters will only spar against it once.

## At the Table

- A duel where half the noise is missing puts every spectator on edge, and the DM never has to explain why.
- Drawing it in a dark room is the whole trick: the other side hears nothing, and the party knows it.

## Hooks

The blade is named for a killing that happened at a crowded supper table without anyone at it looking up. Four guests gave statements and all four described the same thing: a conversation that stopped, and started again, and the man at the end of the table had not moved for some minutes. Nobody has ever explained what the other three heard that made them turn around at last.
