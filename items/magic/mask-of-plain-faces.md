## Description

A soft half-mask of undyed kid leather, so light on the face that within a minute you stop feeling the edges of it. The outside is blank and takes no expression. The inside is worn into hollows — brow, cheek, the bridge of a nose — in the shape of a face that is not yours and will not be, however long you wear it.

## At the Table

- Witnesses are the real security system in any city, and this removes them. The party can commit a crime in front of forty people and walk past the same forty tomorrow.
- The limit is the good part: friends still know you. Every betrayal this mask enables has to be done to strangers.

## Hooks

Watch captains know the pattern before they know the object — a month of thefts, dozens of witnesses, and not one usable description. One captain has a drawer of forty statements from a single robbery, all of them agreeing on the coat, the height, and the voice, and all forty leaving the face blank. She has started interviewing people about hands.
