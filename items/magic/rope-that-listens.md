## Description

Fifty feet of ordinary-looking hemp, a little furred with age, coiled the way a sleeping animal coils. Speak to it and it clears its throat first — a dry rasping shuffle of fiber against fiber, unmistakably the sound of somebody about to say something — and then it goes up the wall at the speed of a slow apology. It has never once made that sound at any other time.

## At the Table

- A minute of climbing rope is a minute of standing in the open, which turns a solved problem back into a scene.
- It anchors to the *nearest* thing. Half the fun is the party watching it lash itself to the wrong beam and having to decide whether that's good enough.

## Hooks

Ropes like this are made in one valley and sold with a printed slip advising the buyer not to give the rope a name. This one has a name burned into a leather tag at the coil's heart, in a child's lettering, and it answers to it noticeably faster than it answers to being pointed at.
