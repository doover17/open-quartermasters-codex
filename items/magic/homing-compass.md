## Description

A palm-sized brass case under a glass face, with a needle of dull iron — and it weighs what a full waterskin weighs, a difference you feel the instant someone hands it to you. The needle holds its bearing so steadily that it looks painted onto the card. Turn the case in your hand and the needle turns with the world instead of with you.

## At the Table

- One direction, forever. The party's navigation problems become navigation *choices*: they always know where home is, and that's a different pressure than being lost.
- Buying one secondhand means buying somebody else's destination. The needle won't tell you whose — only where.

## Hooks

Every secondhand compass points somewhere, so buying one is inheriting a stranger's unfinished business. This one has a name scratched inside the lid in a hand that ran out of room halfway through. Its needle points at no city on the coast, but at a stretch of open water eleven miles out, where every chart in the port agrees there is nothing at all.
