## Description

Brass gone soft-gold with handling, with a wick that never shortens over a reservoir that has never once needed filling. The flame sits perfectly still — no flicker, no lean toward a draft — and throws a light a shade whiter than fire has any business being. Watch the shadows it casts and you'll find they fall a few degrees off from where they ought to, as though the lamp were standing slightly to the left of where you set it.

## At the Table

- Six hours to a flask of oil, and this ends the accounting forever — which quietly rewrites every dungeon budget the party has ever made.
- The off-true shadows are a free plot sensor. Somewhere there is a room where they fall correctly, and the player who notices will not stop talking about it.

## Hooks

The chandlers' guild in three cities has petitioned to have these declared contraband, and each petition is withdrawn a season later without a hearing. The lamp the party is carrying has a guild seal struck into its base, filed down but not filed away. It was shop inventory once, in a shop that no longer sells lamps.
