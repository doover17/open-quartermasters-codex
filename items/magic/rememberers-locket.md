## Description

A flat silver oval worn thin at the hinge, holding a miniature so fine it stops reading as paint. Look at it directly and it's a good likeness and no better — a competent court painter's work. Catch it at the edge of your vision while you're doing something else and it is exactly them, the whole face, moving the way you'd see it across a crowded room, and every owner learns not to do that on purpose.

## At the Table

- A player who fills this with an NPC's face has told the table who their character is without writing a word of backstory.
- Grief with a mechanism: the likeness doesn't fade, which means the owner never gets the mercy of forgetting what they looked like.

## Hooks

Lockets outlive the people inside them, and no pawnbroker can open one — that's the entire design. A collector buys them sealed at a fair price and has two hundred now, none of which he can open. He buys more every year. The shelf he keeps them on is arranged by year of purchase, with one gap left near the middle.
