## Description

A weighty traveling cloak in undyed wool with a deep hood, and under it the light is wrong in a way that takes a day to notice: always the flat gold of an hour before sunset, at noon, at dawn, under a black storm sky. Rain arrives at your shoulder and goes around. Snow reaches the wool and is somewhere else. The hem is never wet, and never dusty, and never anything.

## At the Table

- One character walks the whole miserable journey dry, and the party's feelings about that are the actual content of several evenings.
- The debt is the design. Somewhere ahead is a night where four days of held-off storm arrive at once, on one person, and the DM gets to pick when.

## Hooks

Cloaks like this are sold with the debt disclosed, and buyers take them anyway. A carter on the northern run wore one for nine years and never paid, which the trade regards as impossible and the carter regarded as luck. He has retired to a house with no windows on the weather side, and he still won't sleep through a storm.
