## Description

A ruled merchant's daybook in a scuffed board cover, half its pages already filled. New lines arrive in ink that is never quite the color of the line above, in a clerk's hand that changes with the region — round and generous in the river towns, cramped and slanted in the hills — as though the book keeps hiring locally.

## At the Table

- Every haggling scene now has a referee. That doesn't end the negotiation; it moves it to what the merchant is willing to admit in front of a book.
- Courts and tax assessors want it far more than merchants do. Owning one openly is a political position.

## Hooks

A guild bought a ledger like this eleven years ago to audit its own warehouses, and the audit is still running, because the book keeps writing lines for goods nobody has laid on it. The entries are all for the same warehouse and all for the same weight of the same spice, once a month, at a value that has fallen by half over the decade. That warehouse burned down in the second year.
