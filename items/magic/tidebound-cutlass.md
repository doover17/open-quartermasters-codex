## Description

A heavy curved blade with a basket hilt gone green at the joins, and it beads water along the fuller no matter where it hangs — a slow sweat of it, cold, salt on the tongue, in a dry room a hundred miles inland. Wipe the blade and it beads again within the hour. The scabbard has been replaced four times and every one has rotted through from the inside.

## At the Table

- A weapon that quietly ruins whatever it's stored with is a logistics problem the player will actually roleplay: the cutlass travels lashed outside the pack.
- Put it in a room where the water shouldn't run — a desert vault, an airless crypt — and let the players watch the fuller fill.

## Hooks

Every dockside dealer on this coast knows the blade and none of them will keep it overnight. The salt it sweats has been tasted by people who would know, and they place it: not this sea, but the cold one four hundred miles north, where a ship called something else went down with a crew nobody has finished naming.
