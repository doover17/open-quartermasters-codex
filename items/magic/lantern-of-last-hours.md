## Description

A squat iron lantern with a horn shutter and no fuel port anywhere on it. Lit, it throws an ordinary orange working light. Opened all the way, the light goes the color of weak tea and the glass turns cold enough to ache against a bare palm — cold that lasts exactly as long as the hour it is showing you.

## At the Table

- One hour, one room, once a day. That is a tight enough leash that the party has to guess *which* room matters, and being wrong costs them the day.
- What it shows is silent and partial, so it generates arguments rather than answers. The table will spend twenty minutes on what a figure in a doorway was carrying.

## Hooks

An assize court leased one for a season and stopped, and the clerk's note on the return gives the reason in one line: the lantern had begun showing the room an hour ago *and* a second, fainter room over the top of it, which nobody could place. The two rooms shared a door in the same wall. The court's chamber has no door in that wall.
