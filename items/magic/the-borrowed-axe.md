## Description

A working hatchet with a hickory haft and a poll scarred from use as a hammer, and the flat of the head is crowded with initials. They arrive on their own, small and neat, a new pair within a day of a new owner taking it up — and they cannot be removed. Filed, ground, or heated, they come back by morning in the same hand, in the same place in the sequence, which is why the head now has to be read in a spiral.

## At the Table

- The player's own initials appear. Nothing else in the game makes ownership feel like that, and nothing else makes selling an item feel like a decision with a witness.
- Every pair on the head is an NPC the DM can invent on demand, in order, with a built-in reason the party wants to find them.

## Hooks

Count the initials and you get sixty-one pairs. Ask after the axe in any village along the river road and someone will name it as stolen goods, describe a different theft, and be telling the truth. The most recent pair on the head is not the party's yet — it belongs to someone still alive, who has been looking for eleven years, and who will recognize the spiral before they recognize a face.
