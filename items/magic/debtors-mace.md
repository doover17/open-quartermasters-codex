## Description

A short flanged mace with a leather-wrapped haft and a head the color of old pewter, and it gains weight the longer it sits. A week on a peg and it drags at the belt; a month and you notice it on the stairs; left in a chest for a season it takes two hands to lift out. Use it once and it comes back to a comfortable four pounds in the space of a breath, as though it had been waiting to be asked.

## At the Table

- The mace is a clock the player carries. Every peaceful session it gets heavier, and the moment they finally swing it is the moment the campaign's tension breaks.
- A character who won't use it and won't sell it is telling you something. Let them carry it up a mountain.

## Hooks

Moneylenders in three cities recognize the flanging and will lend against it sight unseen, at a rate that is generous to the point of rudeness. Every one of them asks the same question before handing over the coin, in the same flat way, as though reading it from a card: *how long since you last used it?*
