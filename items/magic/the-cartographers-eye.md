## Description

A flat brass case the size of a prayer book, holding a single sheet of vellum that is never used up. Lines arrive on it in a fine brown ink while you watch, unhurried, the way frost crosses a window. What it draws does not keep: a coastline mapped this spring is already yellowed and soft-edged by autumn, and a river you charted five years ago is a ghost of itself, faded as though the sheet has been out in a hundred years of sun.

## At the Table

- The party's high ground becomes an asset with a price tag. Every tower, every summit, every ride on something with wings is now worth climbing for the map alone.
- The fading is the campaign clock. A five-year-old chart is nearly gone, so the Eye rewards parties who go back and look again — and punishes the ones who thought they were finished.

## Hooks

The Eye has mapped one particular valley eleven times for eleven owners, and the guild that holds those copies has them side by side under glass. Ten of them agree. The eleventh is the oldest, the most faded, and the only one drawn before the road went through — and it shows the valley with a lake in it, where the ten newer sheets show a town.
