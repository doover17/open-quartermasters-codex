## Description

Four feet of undyed hemp, soft as an old bootlace, that knots itself along its length as you count aloud — you feel each knot draw tight against your palm a half-beat after you say the number. Left hanging on a peg, it creaks: the slow working creak of a rope with weight on it, though nothing hangs from it and nothing ever has. Quartermasters keep theirs in a drawer for that reason.

## At the Table

- Inventory stops being an argument. The cord remembers what the party counted three weeks and one head injury ago.
- The count that doesn't match is the adventure: eleven knots, ten crates. Somebody counted honestly, and somebody moved something.

## Hooks

A harbor clerk's cord went in as evidence last spring and the case came apart around it — sixty-two knots against a manifest of sixty. The clerk swore he had counted true, and the cord agreed with him, because the cord only ever agrees with the counter. Two crates came off that ship that nobody has found since, and the cord is still knotted, in a box, in a magistrate's cupboard.
