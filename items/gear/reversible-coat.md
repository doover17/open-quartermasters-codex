## Description

Heavier than it looks, because it's two coats sharing one set of seams: scuffed gray laborer's wool on one face, merchant's broadcloth with horn buttons on the other. The turn is fast — the tailor set every buttonhole to work from both sides, a trick that takes as long to sew as the rest of the coat combined. Only the collar betrays it, if someone thinks to fold the collar back.

## At the Table

- The chase turns a corner; the watch pounds past a bored tradesman in gray who was, eight seconds ago, a fleeing merchant in blue.
- Wearing it to a meeting says something to anyone who recognizes the work: this person plans exits.

## Hooks

Fold the collar back and the tailor's chalk is still on the inner seam: two numbers, the second one higher than the first. Coats like this are cut two to a bolt and numbered as a pair, so the twin of the party's coat is out there, wearing somebody. Last week a witness swore she saw that same blue broadcloth on both sides of the river within the hour.
