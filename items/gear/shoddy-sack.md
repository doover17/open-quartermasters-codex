## Description

Coarse burlap that sheds fibers on everything it touches, sewn with a single loose running stitch and a knot at each end. Held to the light, the weave shows daylight through it. The bundle smells of the granary floor it was swept from.

## At the Table

- The looting montage goes fine. The sprint back to the boat, sacks bouncing, is when you find out what a running stitch is worth.
- A spilled sack is a map: guards, rivals, or something hungry can follow the trail of dropped coin and candlesticks straight to the party.

## Hooks

Grain merchants in the low quarter have started double-bagging without being asked, and none of them will say why. The ragpicker who sews these sacks lost her good thread supplier a month ago — and lost him in a way she'll only discuss drunk.
