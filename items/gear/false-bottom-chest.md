## Description

An unremarkable traveling chest in scuffed pine, deliberately shabbier outside than in. The false floor sits on a bed of felt and lifts only when two brass studs are pressed together — a motion that looks like nothing at all. Tap it wherever you like; the carpenter packed the void's edges with sand so it thuds like solid wood.

## At the Table

- The customs shed scene: the officer paws through spare shirts and a cheap lantern, and the party makes pleasant conversation over four inches of felony.
- Bought secondhand, the first question is the best one — is the compartment empty?

## Hooks

An estate sale is offering the late magistrate's luggage, and one chest weighs three pounds more than its twin. The magistrate spent thirty years hearing smuggling cases. His widow set the price low, and she watches — closely — who buys that one.
