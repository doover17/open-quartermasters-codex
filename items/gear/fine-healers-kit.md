## Description

The case opens flat and smells of clean linen, spirits, and dried yarrow. Curved needles ride in waxed paper, the silk thread is sorted by gauge, and every dressing is rolled tight enough to drum a finger on. One interior pocket holds a small mirror and a comb — because a surgeon who sends a patient back to their family presentable has clearly lost patients before.

## At the Table

- The fighter goes down in the mud, and this kit is the difference between "you'll live" and "you'll live, and you'll dance at your sister's wedding."
- Opening it in a plague-struck village makes the party the most important people in town before anyone rolls anything.

## Hooks

Every kit from this assembler includes one vial the label calls "last resort," stoppered in black wax. The apothecaries who stock the kits can price every other item in the case. None of them will say what the vial costs, or what it does, and none of the used kits that come back for refilling still have it.
