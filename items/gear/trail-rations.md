## Description

Twice-baked biscuit hard enough to knock on, a fist of salted meat, dried fruit leather, and a knot of smoked cheese, all wrapped in waxed cloth. What's actually in the wrap varies by region — river towns pack smoked fish, hill country packs mutton — and travelers can taste where their supplier bought stock.

## At the Table

- The countdown clock of every wilderness arc. Counting remaining wraps is tension no monster can match.
- Sharing rations with a starving stranger is the oldest trust scene in the genre. It still works.

## Hooks

The provisioner's latest batch all came from one estate sale, and the fruit leather carries a faint taste of bitter almonds. Nobody's died — yet — but the town's hounds refuse to touch the stuff.
