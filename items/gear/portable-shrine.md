## Description

Three hinged panels of close-grained wood, dark with decades of incense, that open with the resinous breath of every prayer said inside them. The carving is left deliberately unfinished at the center panel — blank, so any god's sign can be set there — and wax from other travelers' candles has pooled in the sconces in four different colors. It folds to the size of a book of sermons and weighs about as much.

## At the Table

- Burying a fallen companion properly, days from any temple, is the scene this item exists for. The ten minutes of setup is the party standing in silence, and the table will stand with them.
- An oath sworn at the shrine binds harder in the fiction than any contract: the players saw the candles lit.

## Hooks

The pilgrim selling this one swears it was blessed at a mountain sanctuary and carried a single holy circuit, hence the price. But the sconces hold sixteen layers of wax, the hinge pins have been replaced twice, and the blank center panel isn't blank — it's been carved and planed smooth again, more than once.
