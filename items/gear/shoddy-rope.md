## Description

Loose-laid hemp with visible splices where shorter offcuts were married together. It smells faintly of the harbor it was salvaged from, and dry fibers dust off in your grip. Coiled tight, it looks fine — which is exactly the problem.

## At the Table

- The cheap option players choose in a hurry, and the tense re-read of this file when someone's dangling from it.
- Merchants sometimes coil shoddy rope under a fine top layer; a passed appraisal or inspection catches the splices.

## Hooks

A porter's guild has been quietly buying shoddy rope in bulk and reselling it as new to a rival mining camp. The first broken hauler was called an accident. The second wasn't called anything — nobody's found him.
