## Description

The hammer settles into your grip as if it took a measurement first, and the tongs close with a click you feel more than hear. Every face is dressed mirror-bright except the strike surfaces, which carry the honest gray of work. Each piece bears the same stamped anvil-and-star, and older smiths touch the mark before they touch the tool.

## At the Table

- Three days from the nearest forge, the paladin's breastplate is split down a seam. With these, the fix made by firelight is the fix that finishes the campaign.
- Armorers quote different prices once they see the mark come out of the pack. Some of those prices go down.

## Hooks

The anvil-and-star was a teaching mark: a master granted it to journeymen who surpassed her, and she granted it nine times. Guild records list eight sets of these tools. Whoever carries the ninth set either earned it from a woman twenty years dead, or took it from someone who did.
