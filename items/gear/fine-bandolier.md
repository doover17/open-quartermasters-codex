## Description

Supple harness leather with eight felt-throated loops, each holding a vial in a grip that releases to a practiced pull and to nothing else. The buckle sits off-center so nothing crosses your draw. Old stains around the third loop suggest the previous owner favored something that smoked.

## At the Table

- The round where the alchemist's answer is already in hand while everyone else is rummaging.
- Eight visible vials across the chest is a message read differently by guards, by tavern crowds, and by anything that's been burned before. Wearing it openly is a choice.

## Hooks

An alchemist gives a free bandolier to every regular customer — generous, until you notice the loops are sized precisely to her vials and no one else's. Her competitors' bottles don't fit. Neither does the antidote sold across the street.
