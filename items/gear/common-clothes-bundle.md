## Description

A shirt, trousers, a laced overtunic, and a cap, rolled in twine and smelling of the lye they were boiled in between owners. The cloth is patched at the elbows and knees in the way everyone here patches — which is the entire point of buying it. One cuff still carries a mend in someone else's thread, finer than the garment deserves.

## At the Table

- The wizard in traveling silks realizes the whole market went quiet when the party walked in, and the ragpicker's stall is suddenly the most important shop in town.
- Handing a bundle to the rescued prisoner: the moment they stop looking like what was done to them.

## Hooks

Where does a ragpicker get clean, whole clothing in every size, restocked weekly? This one buys from the gravedigger's boy, and a bundle sold last market day matched — down to the mended cuff — the description a searching mother has been reading aloud at the fountain each morning.
