## Description

A squat clay flask stoppered with wax-dipped cork, heavy with rendered fish oil or pressed seed oil depending on what the region has to spare. It sloshes with a low gulp when handled, and everything it touches carries the smell for a day. Chandlers chalk the fill date on the base — old oil burns dirty.

## At the Table

- The most double-booked pint in the game: every flask spent as a weapon is six hours of light the party no longer has. Make the ledger visible and the choice gets delicious.
- A poured line of oil is battlefield authorship — players redrawing the map before the fight starts.

## Hooks

Someone bought the chandler's entire stock this morning — forty flasks, paid in old coin, loaded onto a cart heading toward the granary district. The chandler mentions it only because the buyer never asked the price.
