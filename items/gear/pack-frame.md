## Description

Steam-bent ash lashed with rawhide, worn smooth wherever it meets shoulder and hip. The frame creaks in a slow rhythm as you walk — a sound porters call "the song" and stop hearing after the first mile. Hash marks burned along one upright count somebody's finished jobs.

## At the Table

- The answer to "we take *all* of it": the statue, the wine, the fourth body's share. Someone straps in, and the party's pace becomes a decision.
- A loaded frame can't run. Choosing who carries it on the way out of hostile country is choosing who the party will not leave behind.

## Hooks

A porter's frame turned up at a mountain waystation with sixty-one burned hash marks and its load still lashed on — food, oilskins, and a strongbox nobody's opened. Porters won't touch it. The sixty-first mark is only half burned in.
