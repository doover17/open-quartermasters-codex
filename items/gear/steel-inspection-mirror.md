## Description

A palm-sized oval of polished steel riveted to a jointed brass handle that folds like a heron's leg. The reflection is honest but dim — faces in it look like memories of faces. The back is stamped with the polisher's proof mark and a spider web of scratches from every corner it has peered around.

## At the Table

- The scout's answer to "who opens the door?" — nobody, yet. Corners, ledges, and murder-holes get checked with one wrist instead of one head.
- A mirror also reads as the party's monster-lore paying off: some things you check for reflections *on purpose*, and the table knows exactly why.

## Hooks

The guild sells these mirrors used, and this one has a habit the polisher can't buff out: in one specific corridor of the old sewer works, it shows the corner ahead — and a tall figure standing in it that isn't there when you look directly.
