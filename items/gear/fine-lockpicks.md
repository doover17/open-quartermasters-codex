## Description

A doeskin roll of tempered steel picks, tension bars, and a folding mirror-probe, each tool blued dark so nothing glints at a keyhole. The tips are polished to a jeweler's finish — steel this smooth leaves a lock's pins exactly as it found them. The roll ties with a bootlace, because a silk ribbon would say too much.

## At the Table

- The mechanical benefit is small; the *forensic* one is the real product. "No tool marks" means the theft isn't discovered until inventory day — and the party's timeline for being elsewhere just grew by a week.
- Sixty gold for picks is also a character statement: this isn't a hobby anymore.

## Hooks

Only two locksmiths in the region can temper picks to this finish, and both file a burr signature into the tension bar — insurance, they call it. When jobs go wrong, the signatures tell investigators which smith to lean on. Neither smith mentions this at sale.
