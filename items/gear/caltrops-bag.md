## Description

Twenty four-spiked iron stars in a drawstring bag, forged so one point always stands up however they land — the blacksmith's little proof that geometry holds grudges. These are quarry-forge work: fast, ugly, and sharp enough to find a boot sole through mud. The bag clinks like bad news being counted.

## At the Table

- The rearguard's best friend: a scattered bag turns "they're chasing us" into "they *were* chasing us." Retreats become plays, not routs.
- Sown in the dark behind a camp or across a windowsill, caltrops are the alarm that also bites. Sweeping them back up afterward — one by one, by hand — is its own scene.

## Hooks

A caravan trader sells caltrops with the tips blackened, "for night work," and swears the tar is just tar. The last company that bought a bag reports every guard who stepped on one slept three days — and one of them still talks about the dream they all shared.
