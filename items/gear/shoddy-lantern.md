## Description

Dented tin with a warped hood that never quite seats, leaking a wobbling halo of light around its seams. The handle's solder joint flexes when it swings. It smells of old fish oil because that's mostly what it's been fed.

## At the Table

- Doubles the party's oil budget and adds a flinch to every stumble — dungeon tension bought at a discount.
- The light leak around the hood means "hooded" is optimistic: sharp-eyed sentries may still catch the glow.

## Hooks

The pawnbroker's shelf holds six of these, all the same make, all pawned in the same week by the same crew of miners. The mine claims the crew quit. The lanterns say nobody planned to.
