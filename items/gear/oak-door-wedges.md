## Description

Four blunt teeth of seasoned oak, kick-scarred and dark with boot polish and dungeon grime. The grain is dense enough to shrug off a shoulder charge, and the taper is cut for stone thresholds, not parlor floors. They knock together in the pouch like dice — the sound of a party that plans its exits.

## At the Table

- Two coppers of wood buys the sweetest sound in dungeoneering: something heavy hitting a door that stays shut. Wedges turn "run!" into a fighting retreat with punctuation.
- Wedging a door *open* is the quieter genius move — no clang of a closing portcullis-trap, no sealed tomb behind you.

## Hooks

The carpenter carves a fifth wedge free for regulars, and hers carry a small maker's mark. One just turned up on the *inside* of a vault door in the magistrate's building — wedged by someone who wanted to make very sure nothing followed them out.
