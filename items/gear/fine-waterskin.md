## Description

Double-stitched goat hide with a pitch-and-beeswax lining and a carved horn stopper that seats on a leather gasket. Full, it gurgles low and contented; the water inside tastes faintly of wax and nothing else — a small luxury anyone who's drunk from a sour skin will pay for twice.

## At the Table

- In desert or badland arcs where water is the real currency, the fine skin's "doesn't spoil, doesn't burst" line quietly becomes the most valuable gear in the party.
- Offering the last clean water in your skin is a scene. Discovering yours was slashed in the night is a bigger one.

## Hooks

A caravan outfitter sells fine skins stamped with a running-antelope brand and swears by their lining. Three travelers who bought from the same batch have returned complaining of vivid, identical dreams — of a well, a rope, and something at the bottom asking to be pulled up.
