## Description

Tight-milled wool with a beeswax finish that beads rain into silver drops and sheds them at a shrug. The hem carries lead shot sewn into the seam, so it hangs still in wind that snaps everyone else's coat like a flag. Four flat pockets hide along the inner lining — and the previous set of stitches beside them shows there used to be five.

## At the Table

- The gate search where guards pat down every traveler and hand the cloak back without finding the letter.
- Three cold, wet nights on the road, and one party member wakes up dry, rested, and insufferable about it.

## Hooks

The tailor who cuts these charges the same price whether you want four pockets or five, but he sews the fifth only when a certain courier house sends the customer. The last cloak he made with five came back for resale within a week, and the fifth pocket had been cut out with a knife.
