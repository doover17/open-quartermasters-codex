## Description

Ten square-forged spikes with ring eyes, still smelling of the forge oil they shipped in, rattling together in a canvas roll. The tips are chisel-cut to bite stone. Old climbers can read a cliff face by the rust stains of pitons past — every route ever tried leaves its iron behind.

## At the Table

- The honest trade of climbing gear: absolute security bought with absolute noise. Ten hammer blows echoing down a dungeon corridor is a wandering-monster invitation the party chooses on purpose.
- Fixed ropes turn a one-time obstacle into party infrastructure — a way in and, more importantly, a way *out*.

## Hooks

Halfway up the gorge cliff, a line of pitons marks a route no local guide will claim. They're driven top-down — hammered by someone descending from the plateau above, where nothing is supposed to live.
