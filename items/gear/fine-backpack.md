## Description

Oiled leather over a light ash frame, every buckle wrapped in felt and every strap ending in a leather keeper instead of a loose flap. Shake it fully loaded and you hear nothing but your own breathing. Inside, a dozen sewn loops and pockets hold each piece of gear exactly where your hand already knows it is.

## At the Table

- The night infiltration where the whole party crouches frozen — except the one whose pack didn't clink.
- Reaching back and producing the rope, the potion, or the crowbar *right now*, while everyone else digs.

## Hooks

The loop-and-pocket layout inside is a leatherworkers' guild pattern, identical in every pack they make — which is why a fence in the city pays double for them. Her burglars train on one pack and can rob the contents of any other blind.
