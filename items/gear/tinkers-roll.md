## Description

A canvas roll gone soft and dark at the fold lines, smelling of solder, beeswax, and machine oil. Inside wait small pliers, files worn to their last honest teeth, a spirit lamp, wire in three gauges, and a tobacco tin of scavenged screws — no two alike. A tally of hatch marks runs along the inner flap, well past a hundred, one for every thing brought back from the dead.

## At the Table

- The lantern hinge, the crossbow trigger, the snapped clasp on the map case: nothing in the pack stays broken, provided the party can spare an evening.
- Mending a stranger's kettle at a shared fire buys more honest gossip than a round of drinks.

## Hooks

What did the roll's last owner fix, out there, that was worth a hatch mark deeper and darker than all the rest? The tinker who sold it won't guess, but she'll point out that the mark isn't scratched in like the others. It's burned.
