## Description

Forge-folded steel with a wedge tip ground thin enough to slip a warded lock plate, and a shepherd's-crook heel that bites without slipping. The balance is uncanny — it hangs in the hand like a weapon that chose a trade. Somewhere near the grip, the smith struck a tiny mark.

## At the Table

- The difference between "the door groans open" and "the whole floor hears you fail."
- Guards who find one on a traveler draw exactly the right conclusion. Owning it is a statement; explaining it is a roleplay scene.

## Hooks

Only one smith in the region strikes that grip-mark, and she sells exclusively to people she's vetted personally. If the party didn't buy theirs from her, she'll want to know — politely, at first — where they got it.
