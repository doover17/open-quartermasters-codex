## Description

A dark walnut case bound in blued steel, faced with a dial of interlocking brass petals where a keyhole ought to be. The petals turn with a watchmaker's smoothness and no sound at all. Under the lid's lip, stamped small, is the maker's mark: an open eye.

## At the Table

- A locked box the rogue can't speed-pick mid-scene turns "loot it" into "carry it out, and decide who studies it tonight."
- Buying one is an interview. The maker builds each lock's sequence personally, and remembers every client — and every box.

## Hooks

Whoever commissions an open-eye lockbox trusts its maker with the one fact that matters: he can open every box he's ever built, blind, in seconds. Three of his clients have died in the past year. All three boxes were found open, and nothing official was reported missing.
