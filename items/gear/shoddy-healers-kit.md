## Description

The buckles are mismatched and the leather has the sour, cellar smell of damp that was dried out in a hurry. Half the loops are empty, the salve tin rattles, and one bandage roll unwinds to reveal a rust-brown stain someone rolled to the inside. The previous owner's initials have been scraped off the lid — not sanded, scraped.

## At the Table

- It costs less than a night at a good inn, and it works right up until the moment it matters most. Sell the discount; collect the drama.
- A character digging through it mid-crisis learns exactly what the price bought — and who they saved that gold for.

## Hooks

Ask the pawnbroker where the kit came from and he'll say an army surplus lot, same as everything else on that shelf. The kits do keep coming in, though, a few each month, always used, always with the same initials scraped off, and no two sets of initials alike.
