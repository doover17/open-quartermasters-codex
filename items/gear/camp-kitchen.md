## Description

Copper and tin nested like a puzzle, everything riding inside the big pot, the whole bundle chiming softly with each step of the mule. The pan is black with honest carbon and the ladle's handle has been wrapped in wire where somebody's grip wore through the wood. Unpacked, it turns any flat rock into a kitchen in the time it takes to argue about firewood.

## At the Table

- Whoever cooks holds power no class feature grants: the night the ranger makes the dead merchant's recipe is the night the party finally talks about him.
- Hot food after a bad day is the DM's cheapest scene-setter — the smell of onions in fat says "you are safe for now" better than any narration.

## Hooks

The tinker's stock came off a wagon he bought whole at auction, kitchen and all. The pot's underside is engraved with eleven sets of initials, each struck through except the last — and the auction listed the wagon's owners as a family of four.
