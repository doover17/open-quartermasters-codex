## Description

The veil weighs nothing — a black net that turns your face into a suggestion while leaving the room sharp to your own eyes. The kid gloves fit close as a second skin and smell faintly of lavender water, the way gloves do when they're kept in a drawer with letters. The world treats a veiled figure with a hush; people lower their voices around grief without deciding to.

## At the Table

- Attending the funeral of someone the party got killed, faces hidden in plain sight, while the family receives condolences three feet away.
- The etiquette shield: the guard captain has questions, but pulling a veil from a mourner's face in public is a line even he hesitates to cross.

## Hooks

The milliner keeps a customer book, and one entry troubles her enough to mention it: a woman has bought the same veil-and-glove set on the first market day of every month for a year. Never a word about why, always the lavender gloves in the same size — and last month, for the first time, the size changed.
