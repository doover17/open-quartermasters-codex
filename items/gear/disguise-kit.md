## Description

A shallow wooden box that smells of greasepaint, spirit gum, and singed hair the moment the lid lifts. Inside: tiered trays of tinted paste, a mirror in the lid, dye bottles with wax-sealed stoppers, curls of human hair sorted by shade, and a lump of nose putty with somebody's old thumbprint still in it. The tray corners are worn round — this box has been opened in the dark, in a hurry, many times.

## At the Table

- Ten minutes behind a locked door with the mirror, and the party's wanted man walks out gray-haired, stooped, and ignored.
- The kit itself is evidence: any guard who finds one in your bags stops caring what your face looks like now.

## Hooks

The theater costumer who assembled this kit recognizes her own work the way a smith knows his blades. The new wanted poster in the square shows a merchant who defrauded the grain exchange — and she recognizes the nose. She sold that face, and she remembers to whom.
