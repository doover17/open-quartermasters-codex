## Description

A leather tube dipped in beeswax until it shines like old amber, capped at both ends with cork-lined lids that close with a satisfying thock. It smells of honey and lamp smoke. Scratches in the wax record every journey it's survived.

## At the Table

- The reason the treasure map survives the river crossing — or the reason the villain's correspondence is still legible when looted from a body in the rain.
- A sealed case invites the question every table loves: do we open someone else's mail?

## Hooks

Couriers of a certain trading house wax their cases in a distinctive red. One such case has been sitting unclaimed at a crossroads inn for three weeks, and the innkeeper has started offering coin to anyone willing to deliver it — no questions answered.
