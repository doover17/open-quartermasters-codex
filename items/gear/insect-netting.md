## Description

Mesh so fine it reads as mist, folding down to a bundle you can close one fist around, smelling faintly of the citronella oil it was cured in. Against a lamp it shows its weave like a fingerprint, and every veteran of the fever country checks that weave before checking the price. This one has a single darned patch, neat as surgery, right where a sleeper's mouth would be.

## At the Table

- In the jungle arc, the whine at the edge of hearing never stops — except for the characters under net. Who bought netting and who laughed it off becomes the party's health ledger.
- One net, drawn over the feverish NPC the party is escorting out, is the pound of gear that decides whether the escort mission has a living client at the end.

## Hooks

A quiet agent in the port is buying every yard of netting at double the asking price and burning none of the usual questions on quality. The weavers are delighted. The fever wards, upriver where the netting should be going, report their shelves emptier every week.
