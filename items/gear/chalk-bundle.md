## Description

Ten stubby sticks of pressed white chalk wrapped in twine, shedding pale dust into whatever pouch carries them. Quarry chalk is coarse and bright; scrivener's chalk is finer and squeaks. Either kind vanishes under a wet thumb — which is sometimes the entire point.

## At the Table

- The classic maze-crawler's insurance: arrows at junctions, tallies on doors, a rendezvous mark only allies understand.
- Finding someone else's chalk marks in a "sealed" tomb rewrites the whole expedition in one image.

## Hooks

Down in the sewer junctions, someone has been correcting the smugglers' chalk codes — crossing out true routes and drawing new arrows toward the old cistern. The smugglers assume a rival gang. The chalk dust at each mark is mixed with grave soil.
