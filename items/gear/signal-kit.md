## Description

A flat tin case that rattles faintly with the whistle inside, its mirror polished to a coin-bright disc that flares painfully when the lid opens sunward. The flags smell of dye and campfires, and the smoke-cloth squares are dense, waxy, and heavier than they look. A code card pasted inside the lid has been amended in three different hands, each correcting the last.

## At the Table

- Splitting the party stops being a mistake and becomes a plan: the scouts crest the ridge, and everyone at the table watches for the flash.
- Smoke on the horizon is a question the players ask themselves — ours, theirs, or someone in trouble who saw our column first?

## Hooks

For six nights running, a hill above the trade road has flashed the same sequence at dusk — a distress call in a garrison code retired twenty years ago. The garrison that used it was never disbanded on any list. It was recorded lost, all hands, in the hills it's now signaling from.
