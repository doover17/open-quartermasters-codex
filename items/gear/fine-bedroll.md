## Description

Dense-woven wool over an oiled canvas shell, with a lanolin smell that never quite washes out. The stitching is double-run and the ties are leather rather than cord — the mark of an outfitter who expects the buyer to actually sleep outdoors, not just own the option.

## At the Table

- On a night of rain, altitude, or frost, this is the difference between a real long rest and a miserable one.
- A character who lends theirs to a sick companion is making a visible, costly kindness.

## Hooks

The best fine bedrolls in the region carry the burned brand of a single workshop — and its owner recently stopped filling orders. Anyone selling a "new" one now is selling stolen stock.
