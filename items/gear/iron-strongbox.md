## Description

Riveted iron plate with a rolled lip, a hasp thick as your thumb, and carrying handles that bruise the shoulders of whoever draws the short straw. It's cold in any weather and arrives smelling of the forge. Two keys come with it, and the smith asks — every time — who gets the second one.

## At the Table

- The party treasury made physical: 25 lb. of shared trust that someone has to carry.
- Thieves who can't crack it steal the whole box, which turns a burglary into a chase.

## Hooks

A strongbox came up in the harbor dredge last month, still locked, and the moneylender who had reported one stolen paid to have it opened in front of witnesses. It held no coin, no papers, and no water. It held its own second key, and the key was on the inside.
