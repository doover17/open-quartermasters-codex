## Description

Sailor's gear gone underground: a hundred feet of tight-laid cord knotted at every tenth foot, ending in a lead plummet with a cup of soft tallow set into its base. Drop it and count the knots through your fingers in the dark. What sticks to the tallow when it comes back up is the floor of the pit, delivered for inspection.

## At the Table

- "How deep is it?" becomes a ritual with a payoff: the cord goes down, the table goes quiet, and the tallow comes up holding the answer — wet sand, rust flakes, hair.
- The cord that goes taut *early*, or comes up bitten clean, is a whole encounter announced in one prop.

## Hooks

Quarrymen sounded the flooded lower gallery last month and the plummet returned with fresh candle wax in the tallow — from a passage sixty feet underwater, in a mine that's been sealed for a generation.
