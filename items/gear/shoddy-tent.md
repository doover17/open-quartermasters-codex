## Description

Loose-weave canvas that smells of mildew before it has ever been rained on, patched with whatever the last owner had — a flour sack, a horse blanket, one square of good oilcloth that only makes the rest look worse. Pitched dry it looks almost respectable. The seams are the sales pitch and the confession in one.

## At the Table

- The first drip lands at three in the morning, on the character with the lowest patience, and the party discovers exactly who they are when they're wet and it's nobody's fault.
- The price gap between this and a real tent is a character decision: the veteran pays it without being asked, and everyone notices who doesn't.

## Hooks

Every tent from the merchant's current bale carries the same charcoal mark inside the ridge seam — a circle with a line through it. He says it's a warehouse tally. The soldiers who sold him the bale marked their dead the same way.
