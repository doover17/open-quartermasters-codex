## Description

Stiff canvas boiled in wax and linseed, the color of dark honey, smelling like a chandler's back room in summer. New, it stands up on its own and crackles like a fire when you bend an elbow; a season of wear teaches it your shape. Rain hits the shoulders and runs off in ropes, drumming a sound you'll learn to sleep through.

## At the Table

- Hour six of cold rain: the party's morale splits cleanly along the line of who spent the 3 gp at the last port.
- The one thing an oilskin can't do is sneak — crossing the wet deck unheard means taking it off, and taking it off means the rain wins.

## Hooks

The chandler rewaxes old sets cheap and resells them, and nobody minds; oilskins outlive their owners. The set she sold you has a name inked inside the collar, half boiled away. She'll tell you it came off a wreck last winter — but the wreck she names lost no one, and the name in your collar isn't on any crew list she'll show you.
