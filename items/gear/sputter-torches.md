## Description

Green pine batons wrapped in rag and dipped in whatever the renderer had left at the bottom of the vat. They light easily, spit sparks constantly, and weep black smoke that clings to the ceiling — and the lungs. The peddler ties them in fives with a straw cord and calls them "traveler's bundles," cheerfully declining to travel with them himself.

## At the Table

- The party bought light by the copper and the dungeon knows it: half-hour timers, a sputtering d6 at the worst moments, and a smoke trail any pursuer can follow.
- Five bad torches invite the classic gambit — burn them as decoys, thrown lures, or a false camp while the real party moves in darkness.

## Hooks

The rag wrapping on this batch is shredded livery — the colors of a noble house that dissolved a decade ago. Somebody is emptying that estate one wagon-load at a time, and burning the evidence by the bundle, two coppers a sale.
