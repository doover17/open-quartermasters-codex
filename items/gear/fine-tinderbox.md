## Description

A soldered tin with a gasket of oiled leather, holding flint, a hardened steel striker, and char cloth packed like it matters — because it does. The striker throws fat orange sparks even in a wet wind. The lid's interior is polished bright enough to double as a signal mirror in a pinch.

## At the Table

- On the night the storm hits and everyone's shivering, the player who bought this gets to feel very smart.
- Fire on demand is quiet power: burning a rope bridge, lighting a signal, cremating something that must not rise.

## Hooks

An arsonist has been working the market district, and every witness mentions the same detail: no lantern, no torch, just a figure crouching for a single breath before the flames took. The magistrate is now noting who owns a fine tinderbox — and the party's names may already be on the list.
