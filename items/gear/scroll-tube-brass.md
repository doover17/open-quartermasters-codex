## Description

A heavy brass cylinder with a threaded cap, polished to a shine by pocket and saddlebag alike. It takes six full turns to open — a small, deliberate ritual — and closes with a click you can feel in your teeth. The metal always carries the temperature of wherever it's been.

## At the Table

- The deed, the treaty, or the spell scroll survives the burning building. The building is negotiable; the paper isn't.
- Six turns to open means no one reads the contents in a hurry, in the rain, with the guards coming — which is exactly why nervous couriers love it.

## Hooks

Scriveners' apprentices whisper that one workshop cuts its tube threads a half-turn shallow, so a rapped tube sounds subtly different when the cap has been opened and rethreaded by a stranger. The workshop denies making anything of the kind, which is what you'd want them to say.
