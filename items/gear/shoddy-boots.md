## Description

The leather smells of the tanning shortcut that made it cheap, and the heels are stacked paper board under a skin of hide. New, they gleam with a blacking that rubs off on your thumb. The soles are pegged, not stitched — count the pegs and you can guess the week they'll start walking away from the uppers.

## At the Table

- The party is two days from anywhere, the pursuit is one day behind, and the fighter's left sole flaps like a broken jaw.
- A character who kneels to check a hireling's boots before a long march is doing quiet, memorable leadership.

## Hooks

A garrison quartermaster took the low bid and shod three hundred soldiers in these before the autumn campaign. He knows what he bought — he's been selling the good boots out the back gate, and the cobbler who threatened to tell the captain hasn't opened her shop in nine days.
