## Description

The wallet unrolls with a smell of oiled leather and dried waterweed, each lure pinned in its own loop: bright feather for fast water, dull horsehair for still, a silver-wired minnow for the deep cold. The lines are silk-cored and knotless where cheaper kits are all splices. Inside the flap, a previous owner has inked a map of one river's pools — and crossed out the last one.

## At the Table

- Wherever there's water, the ration count stops mattering — which changes every wilderness route the party is willing to consider.
- An hour of fishing is the genre's confession booth. Put two characters on the bank with one kit and the backstory arrives on its own.

## Hooks

Whoever ties these lures uses feathers from a marsh bird the riverfolk swear went silent a generation ago. The new lures keep coming, a fresh dozen every season, and the feathers are always this year's molt.
