## Description

Three ash legs and a triangle of ox hide, scissoring open with a creak you come to find companionable. The hide is polished dark in the middle by years of one particular seat, and someone has notched the leg-tops so the stool always assembles the same way in the dark. It weighs less than the argument over who gets the log.

## At the Table

- One stool, five tired people: watching who takes it, who offers it, and who it's wordlessly set out for tells the table more than a session of dialogue.
- The old soldier who unfolds a stool at the ambush site and sits, calmly, while everyone else crouches in the wet — that's a character established in one object interaction.

## Hooks

Guides on the high pass keep a custom: at each night's camp, one stool is unfolded, set at the fire, and left empty. Ask them who it's for and they'll change the subject. Ask them what happens if you sit in it, and they stop talking to you altogether.
