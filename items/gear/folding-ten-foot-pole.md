## Description

Three sections of straight-grained ash joined by brass ferrules that thread together smooth as a flute. Assembled, it's rigid enough to lean on; broken down, it rides a pack without announcing that its owner walks into places where the floor lies. The tip is capped in iron and dented like a veteran's shield.

## At the Table

- The classic that never appears in the equipment chapter of anyone's memory — until the corridor with the too-clean floor. The pole makes trap-probing a declared, visible playstyle instead of a DM negotiation.
- Ten feet of reach is also a bridge tester, a depth gauge, a flag mast, and a way to press the suspicious button from around the corner.

## Hooks

The adventurers' guild resells poles recovered from expeditions that didn't return. Guild custom says you carve your name on your pole; the one on the rack right now has four names, each crossed out by the next.
