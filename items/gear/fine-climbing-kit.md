## Description

A waxed-leather harness that creaks like a good saddle, forged ascender cams that bite rope with a clean ratcheting snick, and pitons blued so they don't wink sunlight off a cliff face. The rope runs through your hands without a single splinter of stray fiber. Every strap end is whip-stitched in red thread — an outfitter's habit borrowed from the practice of marking gear that has already held a falling body once.

## At the Table

- The cliff nobody sane attempts becomes the entrance nobody guards.
- Rigging a line so the whole party — and the injured hostage — can descend is competence made visible. Let the climber narrate it.

## Hooks

You can buy the kit off the shelf, but the red-thread stitching is added afterward, one strap at a time, by an old rigger the outfitter keeps on for exactly this. He stitches white thread on gear he expects to come back. He used red on yours without measuring, weighing, or asking where you were headed.
