## Description

The chain pours out of its sack link by cold link, each one stamped with a guild assay mark, and the ranging rods still smell of the red paint on their tips. The plumb bob is brass, heart-shaped, and polished by a century of pockets. Chalked on the inside of the case lid is a sum — two parcels, an acreage, and a difference — that somebody underlined three times.

## At the Table

- Two farmers, one creek that moved, and a party the whole village watches walk the line: a boundary dispute is a duel where the weapon is arithmetic.
- Measure the villain's manor against its floor plan, and the study is eight feet shorter than it should be.

## Hooks

Surveyors in this region carry a bonded license, because a chain-length of error moves fortunes. The widow selling this kit kept her husband's field books, and the last one runs out mid-page: a line of chain measurements that leaves the parcel he was hired to survey and keeps going east, four more pages, across land nobody owns.
