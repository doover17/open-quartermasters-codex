## Description

Boxwood and bog-oak pieces in a hinged board that smells of lamp smoke and spilled ale, the halves closing with a worn wooden click. Each piece is hand-cut, and the carver gave them faces — the mounted piece scowls, the tower has a door left open a crack. The paint on the pieces most often captured has been thumbed away to bare wood.

## At the Table

- The contact won't talk to strangers, but he'll play anyone. Three games in, the party knows how he lies; he knows how they lose.
- Downtime with stakes: copper wagers, table talk, and the one guard who keeps losing on purpose to stay in the conversation.

## Hooks

Look closer at the carved faces and you'll find they aren't invented — regulars at the gambling den can name half of them, laughing, pointing across the room. Nobody laughs at the sovereign piece. The man it's carved from hasn't been seen since the set went up for sale, and his piece is the one the carver cut a blindfold on.
