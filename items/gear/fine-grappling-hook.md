## Description

Three forged tines fold flat against the shaft like a sleeping bird, and each one is wrapped in boiled felt where it will kiss stone. Flicked open, they lock with a click you feel rather than hear. The eye is welded, not bent — this hook has never let go of anything it caught.

## At the Table

- The stealth climb is the whole point: no clang off the parapet, no second throw waking the garrison.
- Sentries know the sound of a normal hook. The absence of that sound is worth ten gold to anyone who works at night.

## Hooks

A second-story crew has hit four counting-houses this season, and the only evidence left behind is a row of neat felt fibers on each windowsill. The fence who sells these hooks keeps a ledger of buyers — and has started charging for silence, too.
