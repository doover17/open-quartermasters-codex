## Description

Carved from a deer's leg bone and polished smooth by years of pockets, with a tone that carries flat and hard through fog and tree cover. Every region's hunters cut the mouthpiece a little differently — a keen ear can tell where a whistle was made by its pitch.

## At the Table

- The cheapest way a split party stays a party. Agreeing on the signal code is a five-minute scene that pays off for a whole campaign.
- One long blast in the dark, from a direction where no ally should be, is a full encounter hook by itself.

## Hooks

Poachers in the nearby forest have started answering the local hunters' whistle codes — correctly. Someone taught them, and the hunting lodge wants to know who traded away their voice.
