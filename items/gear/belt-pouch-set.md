## Description

Three pouches off the same hide, the leather still smelling of the tannery, with drawstrings waxed so they hold a knot. Two are honest working pouches, sized for coin and flint. The third is cut flat and pale, made to lie against the skin and be forgotten — by everyone.

## At the Table

- Coin discipline as play: the walking-around pouch, the real pouch, and the one nobody mentions. A cutpurse gets the first and thinks the job is done.
- Gate guards who find the hidden pouch don't care that it's empty. They care that you own it.

## Hooks

Every cutpurse in the market quarter knows the two-pouch trick, so the good ones have stopped cutting pouches at all. The best one now works the crowd as a leatherworker's assistant, fitting customers for belts — and taking measurements.
